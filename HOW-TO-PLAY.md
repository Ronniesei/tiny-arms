# Tiny Arms: how to play

Co-op dino survival for 1-4 mates, each at home in their own browser. Survive 20 waves of prehistoric nasties, then beat the Triceratops.

**Play here:** https://ronniesei.github.io/tiny-arms/ (Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a PC; keyboard needed).

## Joining a game (under 30 seconds)
1. Whoever hosts clicks **Host a game** and then **Copy invite link**, and pastes the link in the chat.
2. Everyone else opens the link, types a name and picks a T-Rex.
3. Click **Ready**. The host clicks **Start run**.

Playing alone: click **Play solo**.

## Controls
| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| WASD or arrow keys | Move. That's all you do in a fight: your weapons aim and fire on their own |
| Space | Your T-Rex's mana ability |
| Shift | Dodge roll: a quick dive with a moment of invulnerability (4 s cooldown) |
| Mouse, or 1-4 / R / Space | Shop: buy, reroll, ready |
| Esc | Settings (sound, music, screen shake, leave) |

## The loop
- **Waves:** a timer counts down; survive until it hits 0. Each wave starts calm and builds to a rush.
- **Amber:** dead enemies drop amber. Walk over it to collect it. Amber is both your money and your XP. Anything you miss is not lost: it comes back as bonus amber next wave.
- **Between waves:** pick a stat for each level you gained, then shop. You get 4 offers. **Lock** keeps an offer for later, **Reroll** shows 4 new ones (the price goes up each time), and two identical weapons **merge** into a stronger tier. You can carry 6 weapons.
- **Weapon sets:** owning 2, 4 or 6 weapons of the same class (Primal, Hunter, Volcanic, Swarm, Odd) gives a set bonus.
- **HP refills** at the start of every wave.
- **Red on the ground = an attack is coming.** Lines are charges and dives; circles are slams. Magenta blobs are enemy spit.
- **Fern bushes** drop berries that heal.
- **Waves 10 and 15:** a Spinosaurus elite. **Wave 20:** the Triceratops boss. Kill it to win.

## The T-Rexes
| T-Rex | Role | Ability (Space) |
|---|---|---|
| Brutus | Tank: lots of HP and armour, a bit slow | **Roar:** stuns nearby enemies and makes them chase him |
| Fern | Healer: HP regen, ability power | **Healing Pulse:** heals everyone nearby, blasts enemies back, speeds up revives |
| Spike | Damage: hits hard and fast, fragile | **Tail Whirl:** spins and smacks everything around him |
| Sparky | Support: long range, more mana | **Meteor Call:** drops a meteor on the biggest crowd and leaves the ground burning |

## Co-op
- At 0 HP you're **down**, not dead. A mate standing next to you for 3 seconds revives you (Fern makes it much faster).
- The run ends only when **everyone** is down at once.
- Arrows at the screen edge point to mates who are off screen. They turn red when a mate is down.
- At the end the scoreboard hands out titles: MVP, Medic, Tank, Slayer, Hoarder, Show-off and... First to Fall.
